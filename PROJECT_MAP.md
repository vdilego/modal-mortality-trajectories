## Results drafting checklist
- Exact strongest contemporary matched pair.
- Exact `dM`, `dHM`, and survival-to-mode difference.
- Whether H(M)|M dispersion contracts with increasing M.
- Sex differences.
- Sweden timing: when H(M) moves versus when M moves.
- Taylor approximation MAE/RMSE/correlation.
- Sensitivity of substantive conclusions to spline parameter.
